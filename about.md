# <cj>Better AutoDeafen</c>

**By <cg>Blenderanimat</c>**

> This mod is a **fork of AutoDeafen** (originally created by <cj>Lynxdeer</c>), rebuilt with persistent Discord key caching and an in-level **Advanced** settings menu.

Automatically <cl>deafens</c> (or mutes) you in Discord when you <cj>reach a target percentage</c> in a level, then <co>undeafens when you win, die, restart, or leave the level</c>.

### <co>Features:</c>
* **In-level Pause Menu Config + Advanced Menu** — toggle everything without leaving your level
* **Persistent Key Cache (`%APPDATA%\BetterAutoDeafen\keys.json`)** — credentials survive PC restarts, crashes, and mod updates, with fixed Discord OAuth auto-refresh & auto-reconnect
* **Disable on StartPos** — automatically turns off on StartPos (toggleable)
* **Undeafen on Restart, Leave, Pause, & Game Exit** — never get stuck deafened
* **Death/Win Undeafen Delay** — optional delay in seconds before undeafening
* **Mute-Only Mode** — mute your mic instead of deafening so you can still hear friends
* **Near Personal Best Mode** — automatically deafen within *X%* of your PB
* **On-Screen Status Indicator** — subtle top-left corner status badge
* **Test Deafen, Open Cache, & Clear Keys** right inside the Advanced popup

<co>Side note</c>: If you want to disable the deafen sound, go to <cj>Discord Settings</c> > <cj>Voice</c> & <cj>Video</c> > <cj>Sounds</c> > <cj>Deafen</c>
